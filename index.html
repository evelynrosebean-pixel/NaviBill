Basics of Coding

CPT codes describe what service was performed — and represent a specific dollar amount to your insurance. Wrong code, wrong bill.
02

Clinic & Lab Billing

Clinics and labs submit codes alongside clinical notes. Missing or incomplete notes give insurance a reason to deny your claim.
03

Insurance Claims

Insurance checks your plan, your network, and medical necessity. Each checkpoint is a place errors can hide — and money can be lost.
04

Understanding Forms

EOBs, denial letters, approval letters, bills — each tells a different part of your story. Knowing which is which is your first defense.
